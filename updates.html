PocketSage at AIxPH 2026.

Sharing our approach to offline-first AI-assisted triage at the Artificial Intelligence and Public Health Conference.

October 14–16, 2026  ·  Baltimore, Maryland

Conference presentation · October 2026

Bringing offline AI to community healthcare

At the 2026 Artificial Intelligence and Public Health (AIxPH) Conference, PocketSage team member Chris Swift delivered a lightning talk titled Offline-First AI Triage: Deploying On-Device Language Models for Community Health Workers in Zero-Connectivity Settings.

The presentation explored the challenges of limited connectivity and healthcare access, and introduced PocketSage's approach to putting AI-assisted triage support directly on mobile devices.
