Why we exist

Access to information shouldn't stop when connectivity does.

In low-connectivity environments, digital tools can be difficult to rely on. PocketSage is developing a working prototype to support community health workers in remote, rural, and displaced communities with structured triage guidance.

We're designing around limited bandwidth, practical workflows, and the importance of trustworthy information.

The approach

Thoughtful technology for real-world constraints.

Our working prototype and development roadmap focus on three practical priorities.

⌁

Offline-first by design

Run AI inference locally on an Android smartphone, without a network connection.

✧

AI-assisted guidance

Guide community health workers through short symptom questions and an urgency recommendation—not a diagnosis.

◇

Privacy-conscious thinking

Keep patient information on the device by default, with cloud synchronization planned as an opt-in feature.
